Frequently Asked Questions

What counts as an identification?

Each unique visitor matched to an email address counts as one identification. Repeat visits from an already-identified visitor don't count again within the billing period.

What happens if I exceed my limit?

On paid plans, we keep identifying visitors beyond your included limit. Extra leads are billed at your plan's overage rate at the end of each billing cycle. On the free plan, identifications pause at 100.

Can I switch plans anytime?

Yes. Upgrade instantly and we'll prorate the difference. Downgrade takes effect at the next billing cycle.

Do you offer volume / enterprise pricing?

Absolutely. For 10K+ identifications per month, contact us for custom pricing with volume discounts, SLA, and dedicated infrastructure.

How does overage billing work?

When you exceed your plan's included identifications, we continue tracking leads at your plan's per-lead rate. Overages are added to your next invoice automatically.

How accurate is the identification?

We use Fingerprint Pro (99.5% accuracy) combined with first-party cookies and email matching. Typical match rates are 35-50% of total site visitors.
